  - '\N' and empty strings are stored as null; other null values are kept.
  - Rows without a primary key are dropped; duplicated keys keep one row.
  - Numeric columns are cast with try_cast: invalid numbers become null.
  - List columns are split into arrays; characters is parsed as JSON.
  - Missing parent records are kept and only counted (inner joins drop them later).
